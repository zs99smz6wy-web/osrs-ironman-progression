# Guide Writing Style

Player-facing guide text must be fast to scan. Research can stay detailed; instructions should not.

## Step rules

- Start with an imperative verb: `Go`, `Talk`, `Buy`, `Bank`, `Equip`, `Use`, `Kill`, or `Check`.
- Put one action in each step.
- Target 3-12 words. Use up to 18 only when a condition or safety warning must stay inline.
- Name the NPC, item, quantity, and destination directly.
- Put reasoning in `Why now`, requirements, optional notes, or safety details.
- Do not restate the episode goal in each step.
- Remove flavor, filler, transitions, and encouragement.
- Prefer common game terms over full prose.

## Examples

| Avoid | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Get your belongings together and start your adventure heading towards Varrock. | Go to Varrock. |
| Make your way to Father Aereck and speak with him to begin The Restless Ghost. | Talk to Father Aereck. |
| Ensure that you have enough inventory space before receiving the amulet. | Keep 1 inventory slot free. |
| Once you arrive at the tower, head downstairs and deliver the talisman to Sedridor. | Give Sedridor the air talisman. |
| You can choose to run away from the skeleton because defeating it is unnecessary. | Take the skull. Run from the skeleton. |

Buttons and RuneLite current-step text should use the same wording. Detailed source boundaries remain available behind notes or source links and must not be deleted from research data.
