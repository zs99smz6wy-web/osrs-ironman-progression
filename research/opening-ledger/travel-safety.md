# Opening Travel and Safety Ledger

Status: research only.  
Checked: 2026-08-25.  
Scope: factual opening-travel candidates only. This file does not select an
opening route, estimate walking time, or claim that a route is safe.

## Reading rules

- **Cost** is a stated item or coin cost only. It is not a value judgment.
- **Hazard** records a sourced danger or restriction. Silence is not a safety
  guarantee.
- **Normal Ironman** and **HCIM** are separated where death or PvP facts are
  relevant. HCIM status consequences are never inferred from normal-Ironman
  item-loss rules.
- All listed teleports are current Wiki facts checked on the date above. The
  official Varlamore source is included where it resolves a material conflict.
