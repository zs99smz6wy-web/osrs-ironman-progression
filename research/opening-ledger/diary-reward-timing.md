# Opening Diary and Reward-Timing Ledger

## Scope

Factual opening-pass audit drawn from the B0aty and BRUHsailer fresh-start
studies. Checked against current sources on 2026-08-25. This records task
gates, inputs, fixed rewards, and observation boundaries. It does not choose
an order, recommend a diary tier, or assign any lamp.

### Stronghold Fixed-Rewards Boundary

| Floor | Fixed end-room reward | Non-reward boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, Vault of War | 2,000 coins; `Flap` emote. | Floor-1 portal can also be used at combat 26 or above. That portal rule does not create a reward claim. |
| 2, Catacomb of Famine | 3,000 coins; `Slap Head` emote. | Monsters on floor 2 and below are aggressive. Food or another survival method is not a formal item gate, and safe traversal is never inferred. |
| 3, Pit of Pestilence | 5,000 coins; `Idea` emote; chest restores stats. | Floor-3 portal can also be used at combat 76 or above. Restore result and safe traversal remain observed. |
| 4, Sepulchre of Death | `Stamp` emote; boots depend on account-security state. | No coin reward. Full four-floor completion produces the one-time 10,000-coin total from floors 1-3, not an additional 10,000 coins. |

Two-factor authentication is a hard gate for **claiming** Stronghold rewards,
not for entering floor 2. Each floor's portal unlocks only after that floor's
reward was claimed, except where the stated combat-level portal override
applies.

## Diary Tier Lamps

Task credit alone grants no lamp. A tier claim requires every task in that tier
and every preceding tier; the claimant then chooses an eligible target skill.

| Tier | Fixed lamp | Recipient restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lumbridge & Draynor Easy | 2,500 XP | Chosen skill must be level 30 or above. |
| Varrock Easy | 2,500 XP | Chosen skill must be level 30 or above. |
| Varrock Medium | 7,500 XP | Chosen skill must be level 40 or above. |

The eligible skill level, recipient, claim interaction, and XP application are
separate player-confirmed state. This ledger assigns none of them.

## Non-Inferences

- A pass-through task is not an instruction to complete its diary tier.
- A fixed XP reward is not “free” until its skill, item, quest, GP, and
  encounter gates are satisfied.
- A completed quest never proves a separate Museum claim, diary task, lamp
  recipient, lamp use, or retained reward item.
- No reward, loot, survival result, inventory location, or lamp assignment is
  inferred from either proven guide.
