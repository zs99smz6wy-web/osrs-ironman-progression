## Ironman Boundary

Ironmen cannot trade players or use the Grand Exchange for ordinary items. They
can use NPC shops, spawns, NPC drops, and account rewards. They cannot buy from
player-overstocked shops or use higher sale prices created by understocked shops.
[Ironman Mode](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_Mode)

`World hop` means a different world's stock, not an Ironman exception. Shop
stock varies by world. [Shop](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Shop)

## Magic Combat Tutor Runes

The Magic combat tutor supplies **30 air runes** and **30 mind runes** only when
the player has none of that rune in inventory or bank. Each stack uses one slot.

| Condition | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Any air or mind rune in inventory or bank | That rune type is not supplied. |
| Neither held; enough space | 30 air and 30 mind runes are supplied. |
| One free slot; neither held | Mind runes can arrive; air runes are refused. |
| No free slot | Neither stack arrives. |
| Tutor consumable already claimed | Magic runes **or** Ranged equipment can be claimed after 30 minutes, not both during cooldown. |

Source: [Magic combat tutor transcript](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Transcript:Magic_combat_tutor).

Both openings use a temporary ground drop to meet the absence check. This is not
loss-proof: tradeable drops become visible to other players after one minute and
despawn after three, including after logout. The tutor is a replacement source,
but the cooldown and absence checks still apply.
[Items: dropping](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Items)

**Known missing fact:** current Sawmill data lists seven log families, not
waxwood. Current Ironman guide material says three waxwood logs become three
waxwood planks during Daddy's Home but gives no fee. Do not budget that fee or
assume ordinary-plank rules until in-game or direct-source verification.

## Stronghold Of Security: First Three Floors

All floors are free-to-play. Reward claims require two-factor authentication
through a Jagex Account, legacy authenticator, or Steam Guard.
[Stronghold of Security](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Stronghold_of_Security)

| Floor | Reward | Running total | Access / risk fact |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Vault of War | 2,000 gp | 2,000 gp | First-floor portal threshold: combat 26. |
| Catacomb of Famine | 3,000 gp | 5,000 gp | Aggressive monsters; portal threshold: combat 51. |
| Pit of Pestilence | 5,000 gp | **10,000 gp** | Chest restores stats; portal threshold: combat 76. |

The first three rewards total 10,000 gp. Floor four gives boots/emote, not more
coins. Poor-defence players are warned to bring healing and move carefully
through aggressive lower floors.
