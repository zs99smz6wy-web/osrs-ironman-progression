# Opening Evidence Ledger

This ledger joins current game facts to the proven B0aty and BRUHsailer fresh-account openings. It is an input to later route comparison, not a route.

## Entry contract

Each integrated entry records:

- `id` and `category`;
- exact location;
- skill, quest, item, coin, account, and inventory gates;
- items and coins gained, spent, retained, or removed;
- deterministic, player-confirmed, variable, or unresolved outcome;
- downstream uses named by the guides;
- factual alternatives and fallbacks;
- B0aty and BRUHsailer provenance separately;
- current factual source IDs;
- conflicts and model boundaries.

## Rules

- Guide usage is empirical strategy evidence, not proof of mechanics.
- Current mechanics come from OSRS Wiki or official sources.
- A guide-specific entry remains important evidence.
- Shared entries become baseline candidates, not automatic route steps.
- Every material deviation from both guides needs a recorded reason.
- Do not infer bank contents, inventory slots, drops, survival, shop stock, world availability, or quest progress.
- Do not assign lamps automatically.
- Keep player-facing instructions out of this directory.
