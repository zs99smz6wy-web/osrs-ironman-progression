# Fresh-Start Opening Comparison

## Scope and decision boundary

This compares B0aty HCIM Guide V3 and BRUHsailer's proven fresh-account openings through their first Varrock work. They are strong empirical strategy evidence: players have followed them, and their authors have iterated their sequencing. They do **not** independently establish current mechanics, requirements, costs, stock, or safety facts.

The project bears the burden of explaining a deviation from shared behavior. A guide-specific bundle is still meaningful evidence when its account assumptions fit. This document does not select an opening or write player steps.

| Evidence | Use here | Still needed before use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B0aty | Bank-to-bank macroquesting and early Varrock bundle. | Primary mechanics and HCIM-assumption review. |
| BRUHsailer | Dense regional sweep, item relay, and delayed-completion pattern. | Primary mechanics and sweaty-route-assumption review. |
| Current partial-quest packages | Verified boundaries for Rune Mysteries, The Restless Ghost, and X Marks the Spot. | Promotion decisions and wider quest coverage. |

## Shared core: what the guides actually agree on

| Shared behavior | Strategic signal | Current package/model boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start multiple quests before completing them. | Future trips should close several handoffs at once. | Only three partial-quest packages exist. |
| Carry or collect cheap future quest inputs while travelling. | A walk has value beyond its immediate quest. | No opening-specific pickup, shop, drop, or inventory ledger is normalized. |
| Bank between dense regional loops. | Inventory state is a routing constraint, not decoration. | The account model records possessions, but lacks a validated opening loadout and bank-relay contract. |
| Defer Rune Mysteries and Restless Ghost completion. | Unlock timing and geography matter more than finishing every started quest. | Rune handoffs are candidates for milestones; Restless Ghost remains execution-only. |
| Complete X Marks early but delay its lamp use. | Quest completion and reward allocation are distinct decisions. | Package preserves this boundary; no lamp target is selected. |
| Use first Varrock work to start many future dependencies. | Varrock is a dependency-collection bundle. | Most starts and handoffs are not represented as partial state. |
| Finish Daddy's Home soon after its Varrock start. | Free initial-house path has early account value. | POH alternative is modeled; the quest's opening execution facts are not yet packaged. |

## Current-model gaps exposed by both openings

| Priority gap | Evidence | Missing capability or factual set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partial quest state beyond the first three packages | Both routes use many starts and handoffs as future-trip anchors. | Packages and promoted milestones for the selected Varrock and regional quest starts; no exhaustive step tracking. |
| Opening inventory and bank relay | Both list exact withdrawals, deposits, pickups, and retained items. | Capacity-aware loadout, banking, item-source, and resume-point evidence. |
| Early shop and supply chain | Both purchase tools, food, runes, construction materials, and future quest inputs. | Current prices, stock/replenishment, location, Ironman access, quantities, and alternatives. |
| Drop and combat bypasses | B0aty uses rat/chicken drops and Stronghold; both route around hazards. | Required kills versus avoidable encounters, level/risk gates, and practical alternatives. |
| Travel-chain composition | Both derive value from linked movement systems. | Joined facts for each teleport, ship, lever, minigame portal, and fallback rather than isolated nodes. |
| Diary-action integration | Both complete small diary actions while passing through. | Verified task-credit, requirements, and value links attached to the opening geography. |
| Quest XP and lamp timing | Both defer or allocate rewards intentionally. | Exact XP, item/skill/quest blockers, lamp thresholds, and deferred-claim state for all opening quests. |
| Item vocabulary | X Marks package says `functional_spade`; normalized action expects `spade`. | Canonical item mapping before any execution action links the two. |
| Passive activity contract | Both exploit travel-time skilling; B0aty specifies bank-triggered farm/birdhouse runs. | Player-controlled passive triggers that do not interrupt a dense active loop. |

## Corrections to the earlier thin candidate

| Earlier candidate premise | Correction from proven-route evidence |
| --- | --- |
| A direct Lumbridge -> swamp -> graveyard -> Wizards' Tower -> Varrock line can stand as the opening. | It is only one small local loop. Neither guide treats it as the full fresh-account strategy. |
| Restless Ghost, Rune Mysteries, and X Marks are a sufficient opening bundle. | They are shared anchors, but both routes add tools, future inputs, cash, banking, diary actions, and further quest handoffs. |
| X Marks is an optional side branch. | Both start it early and integrate its digs into movement. Completion timing differs, but it deserves first-class dependency treatment. |
| The Wizards' Tower visit should lead naturally to immediate quest completion. | Both deliberately defer at least Rune Mysteries and Restless Ghost completion. The model must preserve staged state. |
| Natural History Quiz belongs in the shared first-Varrock core. | Only B0aty includes it in the reviewed opening. Keep it as a strong candidate, not a consensus requirement. |
| First Varrock arrival is the next direct destination after local Lumbridge work. | Both reach Varrock after additional multi-output work, but through very different regional routes. The model needs evidence to choose an access path. |
| A short episode needs only quest checkpoints. | Proven routes rely on inventory, GP, shops, risk, transport, diary credit, and deferred-reward state. Those are episode inputs, not prose garnish. |

## Acquisition required before proposing an opening

| Order | Factual or modeling acquisition | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verify every shared-core quest's current requirements, rewards, XP, item transitions, hazards, and completion/claim split from primary or current Wiki sources. | Rune Mysteries, Restless Ghost, X Marks, Children of the Sun, Daddy's Home, and first-Varrock starts have source-linked records. |
| 2 | Promote only decision-relevant partial states. | Rune Mysteries handoffs are reviewed; a bounded Varrock-start package exists; no itinerary-detail milestones are added. |
| 3 | Build a sourced opening supply ledger. | Tools, food, runes, logs, quest inputs, shop prices/stocks, and Ironman purchase conditions have canonical item IDs and alternatives. |
| 4 | Model each candidate pre-Varrock travel chain. | Access, cost/charge, danger, inventory restrictions, fallback, and stop/re-entry are sourced for local, B0aty-style, and BRUH-style paths. |
| 5 | Add opening-specific inventory and bank constraints. | Each candidate bundle has capacity, deposits, withdrawals, temporary-item policy, and an observed-safe resume boundary. |
| 6 | Acquire combat, drop, and safety facts. | Stronghold, quest Skeleton, low-level guards, required drops, avoidable fights, HCIM-only constraints, and normal-Ironman alternatives are explicit. |
| 7 | Join diary and permanent-convenience actions to the ledger. | Early task credit, Hans/pickpocket/other pass-through actions, Chronicle, POH, Museum, and transport benefits are fact-linked without selecting timing. |
| 8 | Build reward-timing inputs. | Quest XP blockers, lamp choices, deferred claims, cash deadlines, and attention/passive constraints can be evaluated against account state. |
| 9 | Compare route candidates only after 1-8. | Every deviation from shared behavior has a recorded factual, account-state, safety, or enjoyment rationale. |
