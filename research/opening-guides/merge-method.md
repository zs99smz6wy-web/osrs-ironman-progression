# Proven-Route Merge Method

## Goal

Build this project's opening from the strongest parts of B0aty and BRUHsailer
without discarding setup whose value appears later. The guides are proven
execution evidence. The dependency model compares their choices; it does not
silently simplify them.

## Core rule

**Union first; prune only with proof.**

Every material action from either guide enters the opening union ledger. An
action stays protected until its produced state has been traced through the
later guide: items, coins, levels, quest stages, transport, inventory space,
safety setup, and mobile or passive work all count as state.

Guide agreement raises confidence but is not required for protection. A
guide-specific action may encode a dependency or operating assumption that the
other route solves differently.

## Comparison unit

Compare complete bank cycles, regional loops, and resource pipelines. Do not
judge a pickup, purchase, grind, or partial quest only by the next few steps.

The initial comparison horizon is Tutorial Island exit through the first
Varrock follow-up bank cycle. When an opening action has a named use beyond that
horizon, follow it to the first consumption or unlock before deciding.

## Action states

- `protected_pending_trace`: preserve while downstream uses are incomplete.
- `preserve`: retained in the merged candidate.
- `replace`: another action supplies the same state more effectively.
- `move`: retained but performed at another proven-safe point.
- `optional_branch`: valuable only under an explicit account preference.
- `omit`: removal has passed every omission gate.

## Omission gates

An action can be omitted only when all of these are recorded:

1. Every known downstream use has been traced.
2. Required quantities and deadlines remain satisfied.
3. A replacement source or reason the state is unnecessary is identified.
4. Coin, inventory, travel, risk, attention, and enjoyment effects are compared.
5. The change does not break a later bank cycle or multi-quest bundle.
6. The deviation is visible and has a factual or strategic rationale.

Missing evidence preserves the action; it does not count as evidence to remove
it.

## Provenance and trust

Each candidate action records B0aty and BRUHsailer provenance separately. A
later player-facing guide should expose meaningful deviations with these labels:

- `Both`
- `B0aty`
- `BRUHsailer`
- `Modified`
- `New`

Mechanics still require current official or OSRS Wiki facts. Community guides
support sequencing and practical execution, not immutable prices, requirements,
drop behavior, or safety claims.

## Workflow

1. Import both guides into the union ledger without pruning.
2. Trace outputs forward to their first and later named uses.
3. Group actions into bank cycles and regional bundles.
4. Compare alternate pipelines against the project's objective profile.
5. Record every move, replacement, optional branch, or omission.
6. Write concise player steps only after the merged section closes cleanly.
7. Live-test the merged section, then refine quantities and ordering.

`source-action-index.md` is the import coverage record. Every indexed action is
protected while detailed downstream traces are added to
`opening-action-union.json`.
