# BRUHsailer Fresh-Start Study

## Scope and source

This is a research record, not a route recommendation. It covers BRUHsailer's
first fresh-account arc: **Chapter 1, section 1.1, steps 1-14**. Step 12 is the
first Varrock arrival; step 14 closes its first early-Varrock work. Step 15
leaves Varrock for Construction and Witch's Potion, so it is outside this
study.

## Strategic-purpose inferences

These are analyst inferences from the recorded route, not claims made by the
guide and not recommendations for this project.

| Bundle | Inferred purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Lumbridge start through Draynor | Front-load cheap utility, three quest starts, movement Fletching, and warm gear before leaving the starting area. |
| Ferox through Ardougne | Convert the minigame-teleport network into a long regional sweep that collects food, thieving GP, dyes, bars, quest state, and Wintertodt supplies. |
| Karamja, Lumbridge, Draynor, Wizards' Tower | Close a geographic loop: finish Tribal Totem, advance three early quests, acquire Chronicle, and stage Rune Mysteries/Restless Ghost without yet paying their completion timing costs. |
| Port Sarim through Falador | Treat Entrana's deposit box as an inventory relay while collecting future quest inputs, farming tools, feathers, planks, and diary actions in one pass. |
| First Varrock visit | Use a single Chronicle arrival to create many future quest hooks, turn in Rune Mysteries, buy a calculated rune stock, gain a run-energy tea, and establish early house/quest infrastructure. |
| Shield of Arrav deferral | Preserve flexibility around a partner-dependent quest while gathering whichever faction progress is useful; the guide explicitly prefers the later Black Arm Gang demand environment. |
| Step-14 quetzal and sawmill loop | Pair a Varlamore transport unlock with Gertrude's Cat, Daddy's Home materials, lumber processing, and a future Rag and Bone Man start, then return to Varrock rather than treating each as a separate trip. |

## Applicability boundaries for this project

- The guide assumes carried GP, frequent world hopping, minigame teleports,
  temporary item drops, and a long Wintertodt plan. Those are not universal
  account assumptions here.
- It uses community-sensitive coordination for Shield of Arrav and contains a
  named clan-chat suggestion. Any future project use needs a non-brittle
  alternative.
- It supplies strategy evidence only. Current requirements, item prices,
  diary conditions, danger, XP, and item availability still need primary-source
  validation before model integration.
- Its dense batching is the useful observation. Its exact order, risk profile,
  lamp handling, and 99-oriented food targets require independent scoring
  against this project's enjoyment-first timing philosophy.
