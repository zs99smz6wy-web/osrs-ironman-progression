# B0aty HCIM Guide V3: fresh-start evidence

## Scope

- Source: <https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Guide:B0aty_HCIM_Guide_V3>
- Checked: 2026-08-25
- Covered: `Starting Out` through `Bank 4`, plus later guide banks that resolve starts made here.
- Status: research evidence only. This does not endorse, select, or copy this route.

The page calls itself an Ironman/HCIM route to Song of the Elves, with substantial multi-questing. Dialogue numbers such as `(3,1)` below are the guide's displayed dialogue choices; their meanings are not independently interpreted here.

## Opening Conventions In The Source

| Topic | Source instruction |
| --- | --- |
| Lamps | Put lamps into Herblore after the relevant quest, unless specified otherwise. |
| Combat | Carry a weapon/ammunition; the guide says Magic solves most fights. |
| Random events | Do all except Maze, Mime, Camo/Drill Demon, and Gravedigger. |
| Passive activities | Farm and birdhouse runs are suggested when changing gear at a bank, not mid-quest or mid-Slayer. |
| Bank setup | Use placeholders; coins first slot; runes near top. Set a bank PIN for Count Check randoms, or remove the Tutorial Island PIN. |

## Labeled Inference: What The Opening Is Optimizing

**Inference, not a source instruction:** The guide appears to use each walk to collect future quest items, diary actions, low-cost equipment, and partial quest handoffs before banking. It is not simply “start Restless Ghost, Rune Mysteries, and X Marks.” Its structure depends on a long future itinerary, inventory management, world hopping, a home teleport, and HCIM-specific risk tolerance. Any project episode derived from this evidence must model those prerequisites and deferrals separately before borrowing its geographic batching.
