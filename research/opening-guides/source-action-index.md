# Fresh-Account Source Action Index

## Purpose and boundary

This is the coverage bridge to `opening-action-union.json`. It indexes every
material action already recorded in the two fresh-start studies, in the source
order represented by those studies. It is not a route, a recommendation, a
mechanics validation, or permission to omit, combine, reorder, or normalize an
action.

All entries are `protected_pending_trace`. A later trace must identify each
action's downstream consumers, alternatives, and account-state consequences
before any merge decision.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Source | The source-study section and cycle/step, preserving provenance. |
| Action or inseparable micro-bundle | Only actions already recorded by the source study. Small actions are grouped only when the study records them as one local handoff. |
| Produced or preserved state | State named by the study; `not named` means the study records the action but does not name an output. |
| Status | Always `protected_pending_trace`. |

## Coverage notes

- B0aty cycle rows preserve the source-study order from `Starting Out` to the
  Cycle 4 Varrock East bank stop, including its listed withdrawals and bank
  relays.
- BRUHsailer Step 1-14 rows preserve the study's step order. When its study
  names a regional procurement block without individual source substeps, that
  block remains a protected aggregate rather than an invented ordering.
- The index deliberately records guide-specific branches, intensity blocks,
  temporary drops, and deferred claims. Their presence is evidence to trace,
  not a decision to retain them in a future route.
