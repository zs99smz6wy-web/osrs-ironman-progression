# Pilot Guide Comparison

Read-only comparison of the current recommendation pilot against established, current Ironman guidance. Sources were accessed 2026-08-24. This note identifies gap-driven research targets; it is not a route.

## Policy

Use this comparison to choose the next factual or modeling investigation for an account-aware chapter. Do not import any guide's ordering as a universal route. Community ordering is evidence about common tradeoffs, not an account-independent prescription.

## Ranked actionable gaps

1. **Make quest XP thresholds visible in the chapter.** The pilot reports XP opportunities, but the human chapter should expose the next threshold, quest chain, preparation gap, and lamp target so early training can be skipped deliberately. The Wiki's Ironman guide explicitly prioritizes quest rewards over early skilling and commonly directs lamps to Herblore. This is primarily a strategic presentation gap; individual XP facts still require normal validation.
   - Source: [Optimal quest guide/Ironman](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Optimal_quest_guide/Ironman) (published/crawled 5 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24).

2. **Represent early transport as a payoff bundle.** Spirit trees, gliders, fairy rings, Ardougne cloak, Chronicle, Games necklace, and ring of dueling are individually represented or referenced, but the first chapter can under-rank their combined travel value. This is a sequencing and payoff-model gap, not a factual disagreement.
   - Source: [Ironman guide: Transportation](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_guide) (published/crawled 5 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24).

3. **Gate Sailing recommendations by Ironman ship economics.** Pandemonium is correctly modeled as the skill unlock, but useful follow-on recommendations need plank, nail, tar, metal, cannonball, and GP readiness. The current Ironman Sailing guide calls these major constraints; the official May 2026 poll also proposed courier-task loot sacks to improve early boat supplies. This is a factual modeling gap with strategic consequences.
   - Sources: [Ironman Guide/Sailing](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_Guide/Sailing) (published/crawled 5 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24); [official Sailing: Buffs, Changes & More poll](https://oldschool.runescape.com/polls/2026/1757) (15-19 May 2026; accessed 2026-08-24).

4. **Give the first chapter a real low-attention lane.** The example chapter can report no scored AFK options even though birdhouses, farming, seaweed, Kingdom, stars/MLM, fishing, and low-risk minigames are central to practical Ironman progression. Each option needs attention frequency, duration, preparation, expected outputs, safety, and a stop condition. This is a coverage gap.
   - Sources: [OSRS Wiki Ironman guide](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ironman_guide) (published/crawled 5 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24); [BRUHsailer current guide](https://osrsper.github.io/BRUHsailer/) (guide updated 16 August 2026; accessed 2026-08-24).

5. **Model utility-item timing and alternative currencies.** Herb sack, seed box, gem bag, coal bag, rune pouch, and related storage items should be compared against current Slayer points, Tithe points, minigame time, herb flow, and immediate inventory pressure. The item facts are current but the chapter needs the account-specific tradeoff. This is strategic timing, with evolving factual inputs.
   - Source: [Herb sack](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herb_sack) (page modified 11 March 2026; Silklined upgrade change 25 February 2026; accessed 2026-08-24).

6. **Separate Void requirements from the preferred timing judgment.** Combat level 100 is the requirement for the Veteran Pest Control boat, not for acquiring Void. Elite Void requires the Hard Western Provinces Diary and 400 extra points for the top and robe. Waiting for combat 100 and aligning regular Void with Elite Void is a sound strategic preference, but must not be recorded as a factual equipment requirement.
   - Sources: [Achievement Diary: All achievements](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Achievement_Diary/All_achievements) (published/crawled 6 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24); [Elite Void Knight equipment](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Elite_Void_Knight_equipment) (published/crawled 1 year ago; accessed 2026-08-24).

7. **Strengthen observed PvM readiness.** Perilous Moons, Barrows, Royal Titans, and Corrupted Gauntlet need observed gear, prayer, food, potion, weapon-style, and supply conditions, not only nominal skill gates. Current Moons guidance recommends roughly 70 combat stats, Piety-level prayer, strong style coverage, and meaningful Fishing/Cooking/Hunter/Herblore support; CG requires Song of the Elves and has substantially higher practical expectations. Common Reddit ladders such as Barrows -> Moons -> Titans -> CG are strategic comparisons, not universal facts.
   - Sources: [Moons of Peril](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Money_making_guide/Moons_of_Peril) (published/crawled 5 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24); [The Gauntlet strategies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Gauntlet/Strategies) (published/crawled 5 months ago; accessed 2026-08-24); [2026 Ironman progression discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/ironscape/comments/1uu9m9m/whats_the_best_starting_ways_on_a_2026_ironman/) (12 July 2026; accessed 2026-08-24).

8. **Add a deliberate Mastering Mixology wait condition.** Mixology requires 60 Herblore, while level 81 unlocks all recipes; its herbs or unfinished potions may be more valuable as normal supplies earlier. The project philosophy correctly favors doing it later when herb stock and Herblore make the tradeoff favorable, but the first chapter needs an explicit stock/opportunity-cost observation.
   - Source: [Mastering Mixology](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mastering_Mixology) (page modified 12 March 2026; accessed 2026-08-24).

9. **Compare Sailing-era guide branches without copying their route.** BRUHsailer now publishes a current Sailing chapter alongside a pre-Sailing Landlubber version, making it useful for identifying missing branches and assumptions. The repository should use that comparison for research coverage and source freshness, while retaining its account-state-aware strategy layer.
   - Sources: [BRUHsailer guide](https://osrsper.github.io/BRUHsailer/) (updated 16 August 2026; accessed 2026-08-24); [BRUHsailer repository](https://github.com/Osrsper/BRUHsailer) (active repository; accessed 2026-08-24).

## Overall assessment

The pilot agrees with current guidance on quest XP, transportation, durable utility, parallel AFK progression, Sailing as a new branch, and content diversity. The highest-value follow-up is to make recommendations explain *why an option is timely for this account*, especially for XP thresholds, Sailing resources, utility-item tradeoffs, and PvM readiness.
