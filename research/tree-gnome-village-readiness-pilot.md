# Tree Gnome Village Readiness Pilot

This is a narrow strategy-layer case study, not a route instruction or a new hard requirement.

The current Wiki quick guide lists the ability to defeat a level 112 Khazard warlord, combat equipment, food, and a recommended combat level of 45. It also explicitly documents a Ranged-or-Magic safespot and a melee flinch approach. The normalized action correctly has no numeric combat requirement, so factual evaluator eligibility remains unchanged.

Community evidence supports treating the quest as a legitimate early Ironman option when a player deliberately uses one of those techniques: a current Ironman guide includes it in early questing and calls for the safespot; an HCIM walkthrough demonstrates a safespot-oriented approach; a community discussion values the early spirit tree while pointing players to the safespot. Those are timing and execution context, not proof that a specific account can safely perform it.

Jagex's official OSRS site was checked for current Tree Gnome Village encounter guidance. No encounter-specific official guide was located, so no combat, safespot, or death claim rests on an official source. The factual record therefore uses the current Wiki for mechanics and labels community material as corroborating strategy context only.

The pilot preserves a high ranking where the factual evaluator says `eligible`. Without a combat snapshot it says `needs_practical_readiness`; with a snapshot it says `needs_tactical_confirmation`; after an explicitly recorded successful warlord encounter it says `timely_after_observed_encounter`. No status selects a tactic or assumes gear, spell/ammunition, food, competence, survival, completion, or a player death/recovery outcome.
