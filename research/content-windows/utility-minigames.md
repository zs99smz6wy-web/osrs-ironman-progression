## Durable Mixology Targets

Treat each as a separate purchase objective, not one mandatory grind: Prescription goggles for secondary saving, Reagent pouch for secondary storage, Potion storage for bank compression, Alchemist's amulet for an observed chance-based dose effect, and Chugging barrel for potion carrying. Their fixed resin costs and ownership transitions are modeled, but resin acquisition, herb choice, and value are not forecast.

## Cross-Window Use

- Pair Motherlode coal bag with a declared Smithing or Giants' Foundry pipeline.
- Pair Tithe Farm rewards only when the targeted storage or patch-maintenance benefit is already felt.
- Treat Void as a PvM readiness choice, not a universal midgame detour; the project philosophy's combat-100 / Elite-Void example is a useful default timing context, not a hard rule.
- Treat Rune pouch, Herb sack, Seed box, Coal bag, Gem bag, Fish Barrel, and Mixology purchases as durable account infrastructure with independent stop conditions.
- Activity attention matters: Motherlode supports low-attention play; Pest Control, MTA, Tithe, Foundry, Tempoross, Guardians, and Mixology require active or semi-active sessions. None are unattended-play recommendations.

## Unresolved Evidence

- Giants' Foundry has an unresolved quality-penalty terminology conflict and incomplete normalized reward/mould actions; do not derive reputation or schedule a specific commission count.
- Fish Barrel has no deterministic acquisition path; no route window can promise it.
- MTA, Slayer, and LMS Rune pouch paths are alternatives with different opportunity costs; a later account-state comparison must select among them.
- Tithe versus Slayer Herb sack currency priority is not globally settled; it depends on observed Slayer-point needs and current herb pressure.
- Void timing still needs a later PvM-readiness comparison per intended use. Boat point values do not prove team availability, wins, or a better personal timing.
- Mixology needs actual herb, unfinished-potion, resin, and reserve observations before its reward ordering can be scored.
