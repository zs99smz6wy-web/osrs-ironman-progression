# Early Sailing Economics Timing

This is a narrow strategy integration, not a route step.

Pandemonium is a no-requirement Sailing entry quest. Its fixed rewards are one
quest point, 300 Sailing experience, a Raft, a Captain's log, and 25 sawmill
coupons. Completing it does not require the player to pre-fund a Skiff, collect
shipbuilding materials, or choose a later Sailing method.

The first modeled fixed economic benchmark is a Skiff: 15 Sailing and 15,000
coins. The context reports precisely those shortfalls from an account snapshot.
It does not choose a money-maker or calculate how long the player should train
Sailing to reach the level.

Current official launch guidance and the current Wiki Ironman Sailing guide
both identify material-heavy shipbuilding after entry. Planks, nails, cloth or
fabric, logs, rope, swamp tar or paste, and metal inputs can matter to selected
parts and facilities; cannonballs matter to selected cannon work or activities.
Exact quantities, materials, Construction gates, and optional shipwright fees
are component-specific. The framework therefore displays that boundary without
inventing a material target or treating future supplies as Pandemonium prep.

For a resource-poor post-Pandemonium account, the correct outcome is `sailing_unlocked_no_fixed_next_objective`: Sailing is available and may be chosen for its own sake, but no funded upgrade or income outcome is assumed. Re-enter the fixed-objective check at 15 Sailing and 15,000 coins, or after explicitly choosing a sourced upgrade/activity with exact inputs.
