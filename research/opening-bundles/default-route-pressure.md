# Opening Default-Route Pressure

## Purpose

This is a strategy bridge after the first two union-ledger tracing tranches. It
does not select the opening route, write player instructions, or omit any source
action. It classifies the currently detailed opening actions by the kind of
pressure they exert on a future merged guide.

The goal is to keep the eventual guide reasonable: preserve high-value
convenience and dependency safety, avoid hidden item/quest blockers, and avoid
turning BRUHsailer-style macroefficiency overlays into default chores unless
they produce timely utility under this project's philosophy.

## Pressure classes

| Class | Meaning | Route implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `default_anchor_candidate` | Strong fit for the default route because it produces durable utility, starts a real-time clock, supplies a shared dependency, or avoids a serious future wall. | Compare inventory, cash, risk, and exact timing next. Likely to survive into the merged guide if execution is clean. |
| `protected_dependency` | Not necessarily exciting, but its output is a named prerequisite, quest handoff, item stock, level, or resume state. | Keep protected until first consumer and replacement are traced. |
| `soft_handoff` | Mostly saves a later dialogue, pickup, or local revisit; completion or use is decoupled from the opening. | Candidate for movement or route note after omission gates are satisfied. |
| `optional_macro_overlay` | Valuable when pursuing BRUHsailer-style long-horizon efficiency or incidental collection, but may add cognitive load or bank clutter. | Candidate for optional branch, not default, unless a timely account objective requires it. |
| `risk_or_intensity_branch` | Value depends on accepted danger, world hopping, high attention, or cash pressure. | Do not default until the risk/intensity is explicitly justified by a named benefit. |
| `source_convention` | Guide setup preference rather than account progression. | Preserve as context; only player-facing if it improves usability without burden. |
