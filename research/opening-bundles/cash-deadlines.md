# Provisional Opening Cash Deadlines

## Scope

This is a research ledger for the provisional fresh-account horizon. It records
when coins or purchased inputs must exist if a named opening component is
activated. It does not choose a money-maker, select an opening shape, or create
route transitions.

The default policy is to activate cash work only for an identified purchase
shortfall. Starter gear sales are not treated as sufficient income by default.
All amounts below are account-level requirements; shop stock and price changes
still need to be observed in-game before execution is normalized.

## Required Baseline

The current evidence establishes only a small fixed baseline:

- Budget the 3 gp spade purchase if no free/local spade is selected.
- Budget 2 gp for a chisel and hammer only when the selected local handoffs
  require purchased tools.
- Preserve the distinction between the 25 gp starting fixture, derived 38 gp
  tutorial-sale estimate, and any later observed income.
- Use the Magic tutor's free 30 air and 30 mind runes when its absence, slot,
  and cooldown conditions are satisfied; do not silently budget a larger rune
  purchase.

The provisional horizon does not require a steel axe purchase. If a selected
execution episode needs one, the ledger records 200 gp at Bob's Brilliant Axes,
with stock and an exact replacement path unresolved. Bronze and iron axes are
available as cheaper alternatives, but the opening evidence does not establish
that either substitutes for the selected handoff.

## Optional Branch Boundaries

- Activate Daddy's Home only after its permanent-convenience value and exact
  material handoff are accepted. The known listed cash subtotal is 4,250 gp for
  planks and cloth, before nails, saw, or any unresolved waxwood interface.
- Activate Chronicle only after a repeated near-term teleport need exists. The
  first known cash boundary is 300 gp for the book; card quantity is a separate
  unresolved reserve decision.
- Do not activate a cash method merely to hold speculative Chronicle cards,
  Daddy's Home materials, or a Sailing reserve.
- Treat Pandemonium completion as having no normalized cash gate. The 15,000 gp
  skiff boundary belongs to later Sailing use and must not be charged against
  the Port Sarim opening bundle.

## Free and Local Alternatives

The evidence ledger records these alternatives without assigning them to the
opening route:

- A spade may come from another shop or spawn; no specific free source is
  promoted here.
- A hammer may be taken from Daddy's Home or bought locally for 1 gp.
- A saw may be supplied during Daddy's Home, although the exact stage is not
  normalized; the sawmill listing is 13 gp.
- A bucket is locally available for Demon Slayer and Priest in Peril.
- Doogle leaves and cadava berries have local/quest acquisition paths in the
  relevant quest evidence.
- Magic tutor runes can replace an early Aubury purchase when the tutor rules
  are satisfied.

## Amounts Intentionally Unresolved

The following are deliberately not converted into purchase deadlines:

- A selected money-maker and its expected yield.
- Exact live shop stock, world-hopping assumptions, and sale values.
- Exact number of Chronicle cards.
- Priest in Peril essence acquisition cost and trip count.
- Daddy's Home waxwood conversion interface or fee, final nail reserve, and
  whether the saw/hammer are supplied at the required stages.
- Any combat food, armour, or emergency supplies for optional quests.
- Any ordinary spellbook rune bundle not tied to a selected opening action.
