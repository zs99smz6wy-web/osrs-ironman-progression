# Opening Bundle Candidate Comparison

## Decision Horizon

**Start:** source-backed fresh post-Tutorial Island account.

**End:** a first-Varrock-follow-up full-bank resume point with only actually
recorded items, coins, quest handoffs, and completed permanent unlocks. A quest
start, unassigned lamp, or unverified inventory state is not an unlock.

This compares objective-derived bundle shapes. B0aty and BRUHsailer are proven
execution evidence for components and bank cycles, not the alternatives being
ranked.

## Shared Foundation

All shapes retain the evidence-backed core:

- start Rune Mysteries and The Restless Ghost, preserving partial state;
- progress X Marks the Spot with a spade and defer its lamp allocation;
- take local tools, pass-through diary credit, and inputs only when they serve a
  named handoff;
- use first Varrock work for bounded dependency starts, claims, or unlocks;
- finish at a real bank/resume state rather than a presumed loadout.

Normalized outcomes are limited to the actions actually completed: for example,
X Marks completion and 200 coins, Restless Ghost completion/Prayer XP/amulet,
Children of the Sun/Varlamore access, Museum actions, and Chronicle purchase.
Most opening quest starts are research-only partial states.

## Candidate Shapes

### Lean shared foundation

**Objectives:** shared local handoffs, timely X Marks completion, and a compact
first-Varrock dependency pass. Optional additions need a current purpose, such
as Children of the Sun, a Museum action, or a selected future-quest start.

- **Permanent state:** only chosen completed actions; starts remain handoffs.
- **XP threshold use:** preserve the X Marks lamp; take Museum XP only for a
  declared threshold. Defer Restless Ghost completion unless its reward matters.
- **Cash/supplies:** basic tools and named inputs only; starter gear sales do not
  fund the full default tool bundle plus steel axe.
- **Passive/AFK:** none required; passive loops are later continuations with
  separate access and input gates.
- **Deferred:** Stronghold cash, Chronicle, regional sweeps, Daddy's Home
  completion, utility grinds, and PvM.
- **Guide evidence reused:** both guides' multi-quest starts, deferred Rune
  Mysteries/Restless Ghost completions, X Marks treatment, and Varrock intent.
- **Blockers/risks:** spade/tools, inventory pressure, and unmodeled exact bank
  relay; no surface corridor is assumed safe.
- **Evidence if selected:** local purchases/pickups, slots, bank relay, chosen
  Varrock starts, and the observed resume state.

### Cash/transport foundation

**Objectives:** add one early cash source and one transport asset only because a
named purchase deadline or repeated trip makes them valuable. Stronghold,
Kourend staging, Count Check, and Chronicle are components, not requirements.

- **Permanent state:** separately recorded reward claims, Kourend progress, or
  owned Chronicle; cards are consumable.
- **XP threshold use:** no XP reward exists merely to justify the detour; lamp
  choice remains separate.
- **Cash/supplies:** compare real income to real purchases. Chronicle needs 300
  coins, with cards purchased separately.
- **Passive/AFK:** optional only; a cash target may fund later infrastructure but
  does not establish it.
- **Deferred:** broad regional sweep, Wintertodt preparation, and utility grinds.
- **Guide evidence reused:** B0aty's Stronghold/Kourend pattern and BRUHsailer's
  Chronicle/transport pattern.
- **Blockers/risks:** Stronghold reward claims need account-security and safe
  traversal observation. Opening transport chains have incomplete joined facts.
- **Evidence if selected:** cash deadline, coins-at-stage ledger, cash-method
  safety, transport availability/charge/fallback, and bank resume proof.

### Broad regional utility sweep

**Objectives:** combine an Ardougne/Karamja/Port Sarim/Falador-style pass with
several compatible quest handoffs, supplies, and optional cash. It is justified
by multiple timely outputs, not by completing every accessible quest.

- **Permanent state:** only explicitly chosen validated completions. Most early
  value is partial quest state and retained inputs, not permanent access.
- **XP threshold use:** each fixed reward remains blocked until its skill, item,
  GP, quest, and encounter conditions are met.
- **Cash/supplies:** may provide a buffer and future inputs, but shop stock,
  world-hopping, sale values, and carried quantities are episode evidence.
- **Passive/AFK:** travel-time collection does not establish passive systems.
- **Deferred:** long skilling blocks, utility minigames, and PvM; optional quest
  completions without current utility stay deferred.
- **Guide evidence reused:** BRUHsailer's regional relay and delayed completion;
  B0aty's later Varrock dependency bundle.
- **Blockers/risks:** several starts lack normalized actions; item, skill, combat,
  social, inventory, and Wilderness-linked travel constraints accumulate.
- **Evidence if selected:** named regional objectives; source-linked quest gates;
  shops and alternatives; travel/PvP policy; bank/deposit relay; partial-state
  representation.

### State-triggered hybrid

**Objectives:** complete the shared foundation, then add the cash/transport or
regional component only when the observed account has a purchase deadline,
repeat-travel need, retained input, realised useful drop, or chosen attention/
risk window. It rejoins at the same Varrock-follow-up bank horizon.

- **Permanent state:** selected branch outcomes only.
- **XP threshold use:** claim a lamp or fixed XP only for a named current
  threshold with all blockers met.
- **Cash/supplies:** branch from observed coins, items, slots, and deadline; do
  not pre-farm speculative funds.
- **Passive/AFK:** establish a loop only when its gates and reset inputs are real.
- **Deferred:** every inactive branch, especially dangerous or repetitive work.
- **Guide evidence reused:** shared bank-to-bank structure and alternate timing
  for the same handoffs; guide-specific assumptions remain optional.
- **Blockers/risks:** requires a reliable account snapshot and explicit triggers;
  RNG never activates a baseline branch before it is observed.
- **Evidence if selected:** trigger, before/after snapshot, branch-specific
  evidence from the relevant shape, and proof that unselected branches are not
  needed for the resume point.

## Qualitative Comparison

These are pressure profiles, not a score or final ranking.

| Shape | Marginal value | Delay cost | Diversity | Danger | Repetition | Attention | Detour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean shared foundation | Focused durable handoffs | Low-medium | Low-medium | Low* | Low | Low-medium | Low |
| Cash/transport foundation | High with deadline/repeat trip | Medium when timely | Medium | Medium | Low-medium | Medium | Medium |
| Broad regional utility sweep | High only with several timely outputs | Medium-high | High | Medium-high | Medium-high | High | High |
| State-triggered hybrid | Conditional on observed need | Low until triggered | Medium | Branch-dependent | Branch-dependent | Medium | Low-medium |

\*Subject to the ledger's explicit no-assumed-safe-corridor boundary.

## Selection Boundary

Select no shape yet. Before one becomes an episode, record its exact end state,
declared content-window benefit, candidate-specific factual gaps, and the
proven-guide ordering being reused. Only then validate inventory, banking,
shops, hazards, and player instructions.
