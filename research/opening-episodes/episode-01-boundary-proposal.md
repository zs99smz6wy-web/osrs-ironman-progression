# Episode 01 Boundary: Lumbridge First Relay

## Status

Selected research candidate for execution validation. This defines one closed
Lumbridge relay; it does not select the later route, assert live game state, or
provide final player-facing prose.

## Start state

- Lumbridge, east of the castle, immediately after Learning the Ropes.
- Standard Ironman with the verified Tutorial kit and one quest point.
- Exactly 25 coins initially banked.
- No mainland quest, diary task, tutor claim, item pickup, sale, or RNG output
  assumed.
- The fixture proves account-wide ownership, not a slot-by-slot layout.

Visit Lumbridge Castle bank, withdraw at least 5 coins, complete the local
relay, then return to the same bank.

Daddy's Home preparation. The one-coin chisel is durable utility retained by
both proven openings. These are the only purchases admitted to this relay.

No diary tier, diary reward, quest completion, XP reward, item provenance, or
unobserved dialogue is inferred from this end state.

## Strategy rationale

Both reviewed openings establish Rune Mysteries, The Restless Ghost, X Marks,
and cheap local utility before leaving Lumbridge. The two diary actions are
free pass-through value. The three tools cost five listed coins and have named
current or durable uses.

The relay rejects BRUHsailer's early 110-log/Firemaking commitment and B0aty's
later Stronghold risk because neither is needed yet. It also avoids the
unverified starter-sale total and the tutor-rune ground-drop risk. Returning to
the first bank preserves the next real decision: the Draynor/Wizards' Tower leg
and eventual Port Sarim/Pandemonium bundle.
