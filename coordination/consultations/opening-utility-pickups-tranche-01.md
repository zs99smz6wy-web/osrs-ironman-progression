  Fletching XP, not only item stock.

  Client of Kourend and Druidic Ritual replacements are traced.
- B0aty ashes have at least one later named consumer around the Prince Ali/dye
  routing horizon; BRUHsailer's five-ash quantity still lacks a visible consumer
  in the preserved opening study.
