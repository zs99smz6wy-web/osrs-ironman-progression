## Accepted evidence

- First Varrock is a dependency-collection bundle, not a set of quests to finish
  immediately.
- Children of the Sun, Demon Slayer, Vampyre Slayer, Gertrude's Cat, Shield of
  Arrav, Priest in Peril, Daddy's Home, Romeo & Juliet, Knight's Sword, Family
  Crest, Rag and Bone Man I, Natural History Quiz, and Varrock micro-actions all
  have enough guide evidence to remain protected until traced.
- The existing handoff-selection record still controls model promotion: Rune
  Mysteries Aubury handoff and Daddy's Home completion are the only selected
  first-Varrock baseline promotions in that record.
- Shield of Arrav remains especially sensitive because source guides preserve
  social/faction flexibility.
- Daddy's Home is the strongest early-convenience candidate in this tranche
  because it establishes the free first-house branch and immediate Construction
  utility.
- Gertrude's Cat completion is a high-value convenience candidate because it
  starts the real-time cat-growth clock for later cat quests and death-rune
  trades.
- Rag and Bone Man I is high-fragility only if the merged route follows
  BRUHsailer's incidental bone-collection overlay. If the route does not value
  that overlay, its early start may be cognitive clutter rather than convenience.
- Natural History Quiz is not a mere Varrock micro-action: it produces early
  Hunter/Slayer XP and 28 Kudos that affect Bone Voyage timing.
- Children of the Sun completion has shared guide support, but the specialist
  found no explicit later guide consumer in the supplied context. Treat it as a
  route-local completion opportunity until Varlamore consumers are traced.
- Romeo & Juliet, Dr Harlow, Priest in Peril start, and some diary/clothing
  actions are low immediate fragility: they often save a later dialogue or item
  pickup, but do not by themselves create durable utility.
- Shield of Arrav start and faction choice must be modeled separately because
  the faction decision affects Heroes' Quest partner routing.
- The Knight's Sword Reldo handoff must not be conflated with B0aty's Bank 3
  Reldo work for Shield of Arrav; B0aty's Knight's Sword handoff occurs after a
  later Squire start.
