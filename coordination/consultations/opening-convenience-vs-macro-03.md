## Accepted evidence

- The proposed convenience-anchor cluster does not appear to hide a hard
  requirement for Rag and Bone Man I, Family Crest start, bulk arrowshaft work,
  or broad BRUHsailer Varrock extras before the first major regional sweep.
- Rag and Bone Man I is the important exception among the macro overlays: if
  deferred past tagged monster kills, those bones may need to be reacquired
  later. This is not a core account blocker, but it creates repeat-kill cost.
- Family Crest start is mainly travel-compression at this stage. B0aty's much
  later start is guide evidence that the opening does not require it, though
  deferral can miss later brother co-location opportunities.
- Bulk arrowshaft work is mostly macroefficiency and travel-time Fletching XP.
  The route must separate a small quest-log reserve from thousands of
  arrowshafts tied to BRUHsailer's Wintertodt/Fletching assumptions.
- Broad BRUHsailer Varrock extras such as Romeo & Juliet, Dr Harlow, Priest in
  Peril start, Knight's Sword handoff, and diary micro-actions mostly create
  future revisits if moved; they do not create an immediate hidden blocker for
  the convenience-anchor subset.
- Gertrude's Cat and Natural History Quiz have the clearest downstream state
  propagation among the anchors. Daddy's Home has meaningful early Construction
  value. Children of the Sun remains the least dependency-sensitive from guide
  evidence, despite shared first-Varrock support.

## Guide assumptions that may not fit this project

- BRUHsailer gives very high value to turning movement into lifetime XP and to
  starting quests early to remove later walks. This does not automatically fit
  an enjoyment/PvM-oriented route.
- Rag and Bone Man I has real efficiency value, but the consequence of deferral
  is generally repeat monster kills rather than broken account progression.
- B0aty is useful counter-evidence for delaying Family Crest and some Varrock
  starts while still progressing toward SOTE/PvM.
- B0aty's HCIM framing may overvalue safety stock or redundant preparation for
  a normal Ironman.

## Repository result

`research/opening-bundles/convenience-vs-macro-followup.md` remains valid as a
comparison bridge: the convenience-anchor follow-up can be developed first, and
macro overlays remain protected but not defaulted by current evidence.
