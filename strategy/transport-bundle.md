# Early transport payoff bundle

`transport-bundles.json` is strategy data. It references existing normalized actions and account-state signals, but it cannot add a factual requirement, a graph edge, an action transition, or a route order.

The early bundle distinguishes four durable capabilities (spirit trees, gnome gliders, fairy-ring permission, and an observed Ardougne-cloak claim) from Chronicle and charged jewellery. The latter are displayed only when the evaluator or item ledger supports them; no item, charge, diary claim, quest completion, or replacement supply is inferred.

For scoring, an eligible action can receive at most one named `early_transport_bundle` point for its own distinct durable benefit while another durable capability remains incomplete. Benefits and actions are unique within the manifest, so completing or comparing one capability cannot stack the same permanent payoff through another. This is a comparison hint, not an optimizer, prerequisite, or route instruction.
